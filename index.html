Frequently Asked Questions

Your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) is the baseline number of calories burned while sleeping or resting all day, simply powering your heartbeat, brain, lungs, and cellular regeneration. Your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) takes that base and adds all movement, exercise, work, and digestion.
Aggressive deficits (-1,000 kcal or more) trigger metabolic adaptation, elevated hunger hormones (ghrelin), fatigue, and significant muscle breakdown. A gradual 350 to 500 calorie deficit creates steady fat loss of 0.7 to 1 lb per week while preserving muscle mass and metabolic health.
As you lose weight, your smaller body requires fewer calories to operate. Re-calculate your TDEE every 10 to 15 lbs lost, or if your scale weight remains plateaued for 3 consecutive weeks.
